      About this data and its metrics

      Audience country indicates where the content is consumed; brand origin identifies the market the brand belongs to. Brands from different markets are shown separately, even when they share a name.

      Web: unique visitors and page views. Streaming: unique listeners (CUME) and sessions (SS). Each channel shows the month available in its Excel report. Unique visitors and listeners are shown per brand: adding them together does not represent unique people across all brands. Page view and session totals are sums across the brands in the current selection. The web Excel report's "Total" rows are excluded from the rankings and these sums.

      The web codes caracolco and caracol are kept as separate records, as they appear in the source file. Web and streaming audiences are not combined.
